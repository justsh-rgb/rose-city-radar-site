ABOUT THE RADAR

One page for the whole metro.

Rose City Radar is everything happening within about 35 miles of Tyler, on one page: dated events, standing favorites, and the places they happen. Every listing links its official page.

Tyler has more going on than any one calendar shows. Venues post on their own sites, clubs on theirs, the city on its own, and most of it gets found the day after. The radar reads those sources every week and puts what it catches in one place, sorted by when.
